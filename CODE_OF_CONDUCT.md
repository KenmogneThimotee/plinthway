# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, ability, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting responsibility for impact.
- Protect private information and respect reasonable boundaries.
- Help make participation accessible to newcomers.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained disruption, and other conduct that makes participation unsafe are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project repositories, discussions, issue trackers, review spaces, and public events where someone represents Plinthway. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately to `kenmognethimotee@gmail.com`. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers will review context, avoid conflicts of interest, and choose a proportionate response. Good-faith reporters will not face retaliation.

This policy is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
